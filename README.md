# StoryHelper

StoryHelper is an interactive application designed to assist users in crafting engaging stories. It leverages AI to generate next words, analyze sentences for grammatical correctness and logical consistency, and provide real-time feedback to enhance your writing process.

## Features

*   **Interactive Story Creation:** Build your story word by word with AI-generated suggestions.
*   **Real-time Analysis:** Get instant feedback on your sentences, including grammar and logical consistency.
*   **Correction and Refinement:** Easily apply AI-suggested corrections or dismiss them as needed.
*   **Story Review and Statistics:** Review your complete story, including word counts, unique word usage, and a detailed evaluation of your writing.
*   **Customizable Settings:** Configure your API key, choose themes, and manage story history.

## How it Works

The application guides you through three main stages:

1.  **Setup Screen:** Configure your Gemini API key and other preferences.
2.  **Game Screen:** This is where the magic happens! You'll write your story, receive AI suggestions for the next word, and get analysis for each sentence you complete. You can accept corrections, move sentences, or delete them.
3.  **Review Screen:** Once your story is complete, you can review the entire narrative, download it as a text file, and see a summary of your writing statistics and AI evaluation.

## Run Locally

**Prerequisites:** Node.js

1.  **Install dependencies:**
    `npm install`
2.  **Run the app:**
    `npm run dev`

This will start the development server, and you can access the application in your browser, usually at `http://localhost:3000`.
